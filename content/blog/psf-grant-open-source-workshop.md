We're happy to share that PyLadies Vancouver received a 1,500 USD grant from the
[Python Software Foundation](https://www.python.org/psf-landing/) (PSF).

The grant will be used for a workshop we're planning:
**[Your First Open Source Contribution: A Python Workshop](/events/first-open-source-contribution-workshop/)**.
We're proud to be one of the recipients in this round of PSF grants.

## Save the date

- **Where:** [Improving Vancouver](/venues/improving-vancouver/), 116 West 6th Avenue,
  Suite 300
- **Who it's for:** anyone who can write a little Python and is new to, or curious about,
  contributing to open source

## About the grant

The [PSF Grants Program](https://www.python.org/psf/grants/) funds Python conferences and
workshops around the world. After pausing the program in 2025, the PSF held a funding
round this year for events taking place between December 1, 2026 and April 30, 2027. That
round [awarded 44 grants](https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2026/11/reopening-psf-grants-program-with-twice.html),
and our workshop is one of them.

## Planning the workshop

The grant means we can go ahead with the workshop, and planning is under way. We have
started getting in touch with mentors for the workshop, and the organizing team meets
every week to work on it. The instructor, the start time, the schedule and registration
are not confirmed yet. The plan is a hands-on day that takes you from "I've never
contributed before" to opening a real pull request, working together on a practice
project.

We'll post the details on the
[event page](/events/first-open-source-contribution-workshop/) as they are settled. To
hear about it when we do, subscribe to our [RSS feed](/index.xml) or follow us on
[LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/pyladies-vancouver),
[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/pyladiesyvr),
[Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@pyladies_vancouver), or
[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/pyladiesvancouver.bsky.social).

## Support the PSF

Grants like this one are possible because people and companies support the PSF. Its
[year-end fundraiser](https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2026/11/python-is-for-everyone-join-in-psf-year.html)
is on now, if you'd like to help it fund more community events.

Organizing a Python event yourself? The PSF is
[reopening the Grants Program](https://pyfound.blogspot.com/2026/11/reopening-psf-grants-program-with-twice.html)
with two funding rounds a year. The next round takes applications from December 14, 2026 to
January 4, 2027, for conferences and workshops happening between May 1 and September 30,
2027.

Thank you, Python Software Foundation, for your support!
