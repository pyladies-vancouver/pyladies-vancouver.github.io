The Vancouver Independent School for Science and Technology (VISST) hosts some of our
meetups. It sits on West Broadway.

VISST is a non-profit, STEM-focused high school founded in 2022. It emphasizes rigorous
academics, project-based learning, and inspiring curiosity. Its sliding-scale tuition
brings authentic diversity to the student population. The school also runs summer camps for
younger students, starting from Grade 4.

Our connection is through VISST co-founder Mike Gelbart, previously a data science faculty
member at UBC and one of the founders of the UBC Master of Data Science program.
