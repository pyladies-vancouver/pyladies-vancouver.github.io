## Before you start

This runbook is for an existing organizer who is onboarding someone new to the team. Work
through it with the new organizer, not for them: most steps need their username or email
address for that service, so ask for those first.

For how the team runs and the ways to take part, see the [Handbook](/handbook/). For the
steps to run a meetup, see the [Meetup Runbooks](/runbooks/).

## Where the team talks

{{% checklist key="ob-communication" %}}
Add them to the **#vancouver-organizer** channel on the PyLadies Slack.
Add them to the **#yvr-mail** channel on the PyLadies Slack.
Add them to the **PyLadies Vancouver Organize** WhatsApp group.
{{% /checklist %}}

They need to be in the [PyLadies Slack](https://slackin.pyladies.com) workspace before you
can add them to a channel.

## Event platforms

{{% checklist key="ob-events" %}}
Add them as a co-organizer of the [PyLadies Vancouver group on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/pyladies-vancouver/).
Add them to the [PyLadies Vancouver calendar on Luma](https://luma.com/pyladiesvancouver).
Add them to the [PyLadies Vancouver event on Sessionize](https://sessionize.com/pyladies-vancouver-meetup/).
{{% /checklist %}}

## Social media

{{% checklist key="ob-social" %}}
Add them to the [PyLadies Vancouver page on LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/pyladies-vancouver).
Give them access to the [Instagram account](https://www.instagram.com/pyladiesyvr).
Give them access to the [Mastodon account](https://fosstodon.org/@pyladies_vancouver).
Give them access to the [Bluesky account](https://bsky.app/profile/pyladiesvancouver.bsky.social).
{{% /checklist %}}

## Accounts and access

{{% checklist key="ob-access" %}}
Give them access to the chapter email, vancouver at pyladies dot com.
Add them to the team's 1Password.
Add them to the [pyladies-vancouver organization on GitHub](https://github.com/pyladies-vancouver).
{{% /checklist %}}
