Sophia Chu is a Software Developer working at Improving Vancouver, a local company
focused on data and cloud solutions for software companies. She's currently working on a
series of open source wrappers over existing database drivers. Sophia's background is a
mix of creative writing, psychology, philosophy, linguistics and computer science,
creating a blend of creativity and curiosity for the world. She is also always the first
to say yes to help out with building community and bringing people together. And in her
free time she's a fan of tea, acrylic painting and birdwatching.
